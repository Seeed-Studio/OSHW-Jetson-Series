# How to Install Antenna for reComputer Super and Classic

This guide walks you through installing antennas on the **reComputer Super** series and the **reComputer Classic** series. The procedures differ between the two product lines, so please follow the section that matches your device.

## reComputer Super Series

The Wi-Fi card in the reComputer Super has two antenna ports — one for the primary antenna and one for the auxiliary antenna. You can install one or both antennas.

**What you need:**

![image](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/Antenna-Installation-Guide/image1.jpg)

- reComputer Super unit
- M.2 Wi-Fi card (if not already installed)
- Antenna(s) with pigtail cable

---

**Step 1. Remove the bottom cover and install the Wi-Fi card.**

Unscrew the bottom cover. If the Wi-Fi card is not yet installed, seat it into the M.2 slot on the carrier board now.

![image](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/Antenna-Installation-Guide/image2.jpg)

---

**Step 2. Remove the top heatsink module.**

Unscrew and lift off the top heatsink/fan assembly.

![image](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/Antenna-Installation-Guide/image3.jpg)

---

**Step 3. Remove the rubber plug and thread the antenna through.**

With the heatsink removed, locate the rubber plug on the rear panel of the chassis. Remove it and thread the antenna cable through the opening from the outside in.

![image](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/Antenna-Installation-Guide/image4.jpg)

---

**Step 4. Connect the antenna to the Wi-Fi card, then reinstall the heatsink.**

Snap the antenna cable connector onto the Wi-Fi card's antenna port. Repeat for the second antenna if needed. Then reinstall the heatsink module and the bottom cover.

![image](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/Antenna-Installation-Guide/image5.jpg)

> [!TIP]
> Both antenna ports on the Wi-Fi card are interchangeable — either cable can go to the primary or auxiliary port.

---

## reComputer Classic Series

The reComputer Classic uses a carrier board with a 4G module mounted on its underside. The antenna cable must be routed carefully along the board edge when reassembling.

**Step 1. Fix one end of the antenna to the chassis.**

Thread the antenna connector through the opening on the back panel of the chassis and secure it in place.

---

**Step 2. Remove the carrier board and connect the antenna to the 4G module.**

Lift the carrier board out of the chassis. Connect the other end of the antenna cable to the 4G module on the **back side** of the carrier board.

![image](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/Antenna-Installation-Guide/image7.jpg)

---

**Step 3. Reinstall the carrier board.**

Carefully lower the carrier board back into the chassis. Make sure the antenna cable runs **along the edge of the board** — do not let it get pinched under the board.

![image](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/Antenna-Installation-Guide/image6.jpg)

> [!WARNING]
> Route the antenna cable along the side edge of the carrier board before closing the chassis. If the cable is pinched or kinked it may affect signal quality.
